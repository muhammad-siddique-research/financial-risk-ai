# References

This project is based on established research in corporate bankruptcy prediction, financial distress modeling, machine learning, and explainable artificial intelligence.

---

## Corporate Bankruptcy Prediction Foundations

### 1. Altman, E. I. (1968)

Altman, E. I. (1968).  
Financial Ratios, Discriminant Analysis and the Prediction of Corporate Bankruptcy.  
*The Journal of Finance, 23*(4), 589–609.

https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1968.tb00843.x

**Contribution:**  
Introduced the Altman Z-score model and established financial ratio-based bankruptcy prediction as a major research direction.

---

### 2. Beaver, W. H. (1966)

Beaver, W. H. (1966).  
Financial Ratios as Predictors of Failure.  
*Journal of Accounting Research, 4*, 71–111.

https://doi.org/10.2307/2490171

**Contribution:**  
One of the earliest empirical studies demonstrating that financial ratios contain predictive information regarding corporate failure.

---

### 3. Ohlson, J. A. (1980)

Ohlson, J. A. (1980).  
Financial Ratios and the Probabilistic Prediction of Bankruptcy.  
*Journal of Accounting Research, 18*(1), 109–131.

https://doi.org/10.2307/2490395

**Contribution:**  
Introduced logistic regression-based bankruptcy prediction models and improved probabilistic interpretation of financial distress.
